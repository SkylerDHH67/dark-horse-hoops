Board link generator

Turns your Google Sheet tab links into one darkhorsehoops.net board link. This page is private — it isn't linked from the public site. Bookmark it for your own use.

Before you paste anything: the Sheet must be shared as Anyone with the link → Viewer (Share button, top right). That makes the whole spreadsheet fetchable by anyone who has a link to any tab of it — including Internal tabs, if someone ever gets or guesses that tab's link. Keep anything you wouldn't want a stranger to see (Skyler Notes, Gemini prompts, etc.) in mind before sharing this way.

Once it's shared, just open each tab (Report Brief - Published and Players - Published) and copy its normal address-bar URL — no "Publish to web" step needed. (Old-style "Publish to web" CSV links still work too, if you'd rather keep Internal tabs fully unreachable regardless of gid.)
