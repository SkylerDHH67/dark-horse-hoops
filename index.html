What Dark Horse Hoops is

Discovery, filtering, and fit — nothing else

We are not trying to replace the parts of your organization that already work. We handle the part that eats the most time: finding out who's actually worth a closer look.

What we do

  • Understand the specific roster need
  • Identify players who could plausibly solve it
  • Build a curated board with film and basketball context
  • Give concise reasoning for why each player is worth your time

What we don't do

  • Replace your scouts or general manager
  • Replace coaching or development decisions
  • Run character references or negotiations
  • Act as an agent
Process

From roster need to shortlist

Three steps, one board, delivered as a link you can open on any device.

01

You tell us the need

Position, role, style of play, budget range, timeline — whatever defines the search.

02

We identify players who plausibly fit, and build the reasoning behind each one.

03

You evaluate

A private board link with film, context, and fit — ready for your own deeper look.

What you receive
